Our Story

We met in the old-fashioned way in 2019: through friends who thought we should meet.

For a while, we both insisted we were only friends — at least to our friends who introduced us. Perhaps we were simply the last to see what everyone else already could.

Seven years, two relocations, and one very patient dog later, Ponzing asked the question on a quiet Sunday morning back in Jos. No cameras. No crowd. No ring. Just the two of them, like a true bush Tarok man.

Now, they can’t wait to do it all over again, this time surrounded by everyone they care about, with all the people who have been part of their story there to witness the next chapter.

Travel Guide

Arrival

Yakubu Gowon Airport (JOS) has limited direct flights, so most guests connect through Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja, then a short domestic hop or a scenic 3-hour drive up to Jos.

By Road

Travelling by bus? These interstate lines run safe, reliable routes into Jos:

Valgee Nengee Bluewhales
Bolt in Jos
  1. Install the Bolt app and add a card before you arrive
  2. Availability is thinner than in bigger cities, especially outside the town centre — book a little ahead
  3. Confirm the fare in-app before your driver starts moving

Getting Around

Bolt covers most of the city, but a hired driver for the weekend is the most relaxed option if you're staying a few days — several hotels can arrange one. The plateau air means cooler evenings, so pack a light jacket.
