Our Story

It started, as these things often do in Jos, at someone else's party.

Ponzing was the friend of the groom at a mutual friend's owambe in 2019 — the one who kept refilling everyone's drinks and remembering names. Glory was the one who noticed. Three dances and one very long conversation about jollof rice later, they exchanged numbers and never really stopped talking.

Six years, two relocations, and one very patient dog later, Ponzing asked the question on a Sunday morning back in Jos — no cameras, no crowd, just the two of them and a very good cup of coffee. Now they can't wait to do it again, this time with everyone they love watching.

Logistics & Recommendations

Travel Guide

Arrival

Yakubu Gowon Airport (JOS) has limited direct flights, so most guests connect through Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja, then a short domestic hop or a scenic 3-hour drive up to Jos.

Bolt in Jos
  1. Install the Bolt app and add a card before you arrive
  2. Availability is thinner than in bigger cities, especially outside the town centre — book a little ahead
  3. Confirm the fare in-app before your driver starts moving

Getting Around

Bolt covers most of the city, but a hired driver for the weekend is the most relaxed option if you're staying a few days — several hotels can arrange one. The plateau air means cooler evenings, so pack a light jacket.
